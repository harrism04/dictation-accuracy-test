Getting a fair result

  • Turn off AI cleanup / rewriting in your dictation app to measure raw transcription. Turn it back on to measure what you'd actually get.
  • Do 3+ takes per model on the same passage. One take is noisy.
  • Keep mic, distance and room the same between models.
  • Stutter-forgiving removes fillers (um, uh), repeated words and part-word starts (“p-p-picked”) before scoring, so an engine isn’t punished for faithfully writing a stutter. Strict counts everything.
  • Everything is stored only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
